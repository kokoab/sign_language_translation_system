What changed historically
1. October 8 replaced LocalA with a different shared parent/phrase-adaptation lineage.
   Original/v1/v2 MY and LIKE local manifest rows were identical. Other classes' data,
   parent initialization, phrase mixture and objectives changed. A shared classifier is
   not one independent model per sign: unchanged MY files do not preserve MY decisions.
   Prior identical-input tests locate the first MY loss before variant filtering, so
   blaming only removed clips or only the later HAVE rules is unsupported.
2. October 9/10 fragment/reject supervision changed treatment of incomplete windows;
   runtime dip bridging, confidence thresholds and commitment delays changed as well.
3. A global ordering change in an earlier HAVE fix also delayed NAME-mode entry. That
   fault was demonstrated separately and removed by the current guarded rollback.
4. The installed MY rule is one-way LIKE-to-MY refinement of candidate scores. It never
   deliberately rewrites a model MY to LIKE. It requires positive flat contact and an
   observed unpinched tail, capped at the next decoded sign. The physical transition can
   start BEFORE that decoded boundary: forming FRIEND closes the fingers and defeats the
   MY rescue. Unknown evidence retains the classifier; short MY can also be swallowed by
   a later segmentation path. Passing isolated clips did not validate all these transitions.

Implementation correction discovered during the port
The old word decoder accepts scores >=0.5. The existing HAVE/ANGRY guard redistributes
the pair's probability mass using geometry. It can select HAVE yet reduce the winning
score below0.5. In three first-contact replay schedules, ANGRY therefore became silent.
The rank variant changes only this adjustment to swap the HAVE/ANGRY logits when the
existing positive geometry eligibility passes. It preserves the winning score and other
class logits. TIRED already uses a score swap. These scores are not claimed calibrated
probabilities after geometry refinement. Example arithmetic, not a measured event:
ANGRY0.7+HAVE0.1 with geometry0.6 gives HAVE0.48 under redistribution, below0.5;
a swap retains0.7. New rank evidence:53/56reviewed HAVE contacts versus49/56baseline
and50/56directguard, withthreeoriginal omissions remaining. MY outputs are unchanged.

Decision and next repair
Keep the score-preserving HAVE patch as a measured report-local improvement on the
older baseline. Do not install any tested whole-model rollback as a no-regression fix.
The unresolved MY repair must distinguish completed MY from the hand transition into
FRIEND/NAME and from genuine LIKE continuation, with candidate boundaries and commitment
validated together. A flat contact followed by closure is insufficient by itself: the
closure can belong to the next sign. Earlier positive-MY cues also occur in genuineLIKE,
so forcing every transientMY tocommit is not justified. Existing isolated controls must
be complemented by deliberate LIKE-to-next-sign controls before such a temporal policy
is promoted. The eight validation MY NAME videos stay out of training. Pin the older
checkpoint as a recovery reference; do not replace its whole shared training lineage
while claiming the unaffected glosses are unchanged.

No general zero-regression guarantee, complete MY/HAVE cure, normal-iPhone test of this
port, new training, or immutable exactSeptember30full-stack restoration is claimed.
All new comparisons were nativeMac/CoreMLreplays; phone evidence was pulled read-only.

match. Fullcontrol/verifierchecks pass; initial threshold-mismatch outputs are archived
