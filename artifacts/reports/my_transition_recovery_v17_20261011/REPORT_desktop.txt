WHY IT REGRESSED
1. The October replacements changed shared weights, parent initialization, phrase supervision and later fragment/reject objectives. Unchanged isolated MY/LIKE files did not freeze those decisions. Historical phrase MY supervision changed from 308 spans/32 videos to 224/24; eight MY NAME videos were excluded from training to enforce previously assigned validation roles. This was a leakage correction, not deletion of the source videos. Historical trainers also changed shared encoders. Earlier identical-input tests locate MY loss before variant filtering, so the HAVE geometry rule alone does not explain the regression.
2. Short MY in a phrase reaches the recognizer in a different window than a clean isolated MY. The following FRIEND/NAME/EAT motion enters the context. The prior clean-core fit did not test this: the current head got only 24/89 exported actual MY windows right. Cutting future context helped MY but hurt genuine LIKE, ruling out a global crop change.
3. Segmentation/commitment matters even when the classifier says MY. A larger following-sign span can absorb the short contact. Increasing MY confidence indiscriminately changed the decoding path and even introduced unrelated words on a DRINK control. True LIKE can also begin with a flat MY-looking prefix, so 'no pinch yet' is insufficient evidence for an early MY.
4. Restoring old supervision alone is not a demonstrated cure. Six bounded restoration arms on the eight user-authorized historical sources improved the actual-window diagnostic only 24/89 to 25/89 and failed retention. The best repair instead uses actual transition inputs. This does not prove every possible full retraining or old split is useless; it rejects the tested restoration-only recipe.

RUNTIME EXPERIMENTS AND REJECTIONS
- September/old-expert rollback and alternative window context: useful partial recovery; neither a safe complete replacement nor a complete MY repair. Old-expert90% fallback recovered42/64 zero-offset pairs with controls retained.
- Twelve movement-completion variants and old/current alternative-window rescoring: insufficient recovery and/or false MY on genuine LIKE.
- Full adapted-head replacement: catches MY but swallows NAME and harms LIKE/other streaming clips. Rejected.
- Bounded/scoped one-way MY updates, probability mixtures, rank swaps, calibration and successor confirmation: progressively improve54–63/64 original pairs. Some variants introduce DRINK MOTHER; rejected.
- Confidence-preserving paired head: 64/64 original pairs, but only176/192 after capture offsets. Confidence-aware head raises this to182/192 while retaining570/59 controls. This exposes why eight favorable replay phases were insufficient.
- Broad boundary insertion:21/24 full sequences, but false MY prefixes in HAPPY/PLEASE/FEEL. Rejected. Positive stationary contact removes most; requiring distinct following handshape removes all eight focused failures.
- Three-contact-row confirmation misses a very brief MY when only two reliable observations survive. Two stationary rows at least30ms apart plus bounded observed follow-through restores those cases; all32 negative-fixture/configuration combinations retained baseline outputs.
- Broad successor confidence boosting repairs one FRIEND/WRITE split but creates WRITE on three other schedules. Rejected even though570cached,59genuine and66synthetic controls retain their recognized sequences. Its historical sweep was explicitly stopped after rejection; partial output is preserved and never counted as a completed gate.
- Final narrow merge: only strengthens a highly confident whole candidate covering two conflicting decoded pieces whose first-piece adapted identity agrees. It preserves a genuine trailing WRITE on24/24 synthetic controls and fixes the spurious WRITE in the target replays. No FRIEND/NAME/EAT label whitelist or removal of the word WRITE.

FINAL REPAIR IMPLEMENTATION
A dual-output recognizer shares the frozen encoders and retains the original word head plus original letter head. The trained head supplies narrowly scoped transition evidence. Original word AND letter outputs are bit-exact against the installed FP16 model for all378parity inputs (maximum absolute difference0). The original word head still feeds the letter head.

1. Positive MY correction requires adapted MY evidence, short plausible chest contact or an independently recognized following gesture. It preserves the original non-MY relative distribution and avoids indiscriminately boosting already-MY spans.
2. A swallowed MY gets a local boundary hint only after positive stationary flat contact and a distinct observed following handshape; missing landmarks do not authorize inserting MY. The contact episode is bounded, preventing a slice of a long held pose/rub from automatically becoming MY.
3. The immediate successor can use confident adapted identity; a whole-span merge requires agreement across whole-gesture and first-piece evidence. Other scores are not globally replaced. Whole-successor score correction preserves total word-score mass and every letter score.

Exact source/model/parameters: candidate_source_manifest.json. Native code: merge_native/. Model: SpanRecognizerV17ConfidenceDualB8FP16.mlpackage. Parameters include MYfloor0.5, short-span350ms, agreement0.9, stationary range0.06shoulder-widths, at least2rows/30ms, maximum contact350ms, following-shape window350ms, local onsetfloor0.4, successor identityfloor0.85/gap600ms, whole-mergefloor0.995. These are tested development settings, not universal ASL definitions.
