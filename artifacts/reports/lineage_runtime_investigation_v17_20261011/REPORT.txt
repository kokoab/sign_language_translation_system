Proposed repair, with separate acceptance gates
---------------------------------------------
1. Freeze this replay pack and add true LIKE plus ANGRY/TIRED and BIG/LANGUAGE controls,
   preserving reviewed intent. First repair observability: capture all modes, resets,
   auxiliary sampling and actual processed timestamps. Diagnostic recordings used for
   selection remain development data. Keep a separate subsequent phone confirmation set.
2. Use the existing old clean-split interval recognizer as the first recovery candidate;
   prepare its matched letter head/export and compare it with the current model in the
   same full runtime. Passing one span or a whole-clip score is not permission to deploy.
3. Target temporal recognition: bounded adaptation on reviewed real sign cores with genuine
   neighboring transitions, realistic short durations and auxiliary sampling-phase variation.
   Retain correctly labeled full-sign/LIKE controls and full100-class replay. Correct variant
   exclusions per clip. Pin the recipe/inputs/split hashes before any training; do not bypass
   canonical training_ready=false or treat the scope-limited recipe as blanket permission.
   Do not label arbitrary fragments as full signs or infer intent from the model's outputs.
4. Repair decoder state separately: stable word-state semantics through NAME mode and a
   general tested policy for retaining valid short-sign evidence as the following sign starts.
   Preserve chronological publication/endpoints; include HELP duplicate and NAME-order tests.
   Shape/roll cues may remain bounded checks, but the previously failed MY relabel heuristics
   and timer/threshold-only changes are not the proposed repair.
5. Promotion requires MY FRIEND/MY NAME/I HAVE BIG plus genuine LIKE/ANGRY/TIRED and
   BIG/LANGUAGE controls to survive repeated phase/timestamp schedules, with acceptable
   per-class full-vocabulary retention, then normal loaded-app tests on the physical phone.
   Evaluate model-only and decoder-only changes separately before combining them. Record
   every substitution AND omission; fewer wrong words achieved by silence is not success.

This is an investigation and proposed repair, not a claim that all signs are now fixed.
No existing tested configuration meets the complete gate. The model repair and temporal
state repair need independent evidence; neither training alone nor rollback alone is proven.
