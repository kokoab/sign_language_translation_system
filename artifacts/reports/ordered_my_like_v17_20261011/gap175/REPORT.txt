WHY THE FIRST PHONE TEST FAILED
The original 150ms version failed both candidate passes. At the first MY, reliable thumb/middle evidence jumped from 2.9s to 3.0667s, a 166.7ms gap. Frames at 2.9667s and 3.0333s were processed, but their landmarks did not meet the cue's confidence requirements. The rule therefore correctly followed its configured uncertainty policy and retained LIKE. A control pass that happened to include reliable evidence at 3.0s had only a 100ms gap and would have accepted MY. The difference is observable in phone_gap_diagnostic.json, not inferred from model replacement or temperature.

BOUNDED FOLLOW-UP
Changed only the maximum reliable-landmark gap from 150ms to 175ms. Original timestamps, positive flat-chest evidence, ordered pinch/departure check, next-word boundary, MY-top-three restriction, one-way correction, and unknown-preserves-model policy remain unchanged. This tolerance was chosen using this known failure, so the follow-up is development evidence, not independent validation. It is still not safe to interpret a long occlusion as absence of LIKE's pinch.
